The approach

How a chapter goes from scary to solved.

01

See it before you solve it

Each topic starts with the picture or the reason behind it. Once you know why a formula works, you stop forgetting it.

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ → why the graph has asymptotes
02

Learn the question types

Cambridge asks the same ideas in a handful of recurring shapes. You learn to recognise each shape and the first line that unlocks it.

"Show that…" · "Hence…" · "Find the range of k…"
03

Drill with real papers

Practice comes straight from past papers, topic by topic first, then full timed papers as the exam gets close.

9709/32 · 4037/12 · 4024/22 → timed, marked, reviewed
04

Think like the examiner

Mark schemes and examiner reports show where marks are won and lost. You learn to write the working that collects every method mark.

M1 A1 B1 → method, accuracy, independent
